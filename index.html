    Made by Anastasia Abakumova and Claude Code, Anthropic’s AI coding assistant. The data belongs to Anastasia.

    Data: Mi Band bracelets via Mi Fit / Zepp (2016–2021) and Mi Fitness (2017–2026) exports, normalized by scripts/normalize.py.

    Heart rate starts in September 2018. Calories are left out because older and newer bands count them differently. Deep-sleep estimates differ between band generations. Nights shorter than 2 h or longer than 14 h (an unworn band scored as sleep) are left out of sleep figures. Fat and lean mass are the smart scale’s estimates; readings taken during pregnancy are left out.
